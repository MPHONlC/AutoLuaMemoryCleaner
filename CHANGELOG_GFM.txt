### Version 2026.10.07.10.43 <sub>*(10/07/2026)*</sub>

#### Memory Cleanup
* Cleanups from other add-ons that use <kbd>LibAPH</kbd> show in the window, chat logs and
  announcements.

#### Slash Commands
* <kbd>/alcon</kbd>, <kbd>/alccsa</kbd>, <kbd>/alclogs</kbd>, <kbd>/alcui</kbd> and
  <kbd>/alclock</kbd> now say what they switched on or off.
* <kbd>/alclock</kbd> and <kbd>/alcreset</kbd> are hidden while Show UI is off, and
  <kbd>/alcpoolconfirm</kbd> while <kbd>Auto Pool Cleanup After Travel</kbd> is off.

#### Settings Menu
* The status window settings and the <kbd>Auto Pool Cleanup After Travel</kbd>
  Confirmation checkbox only appear while their setting is on.
